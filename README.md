# Jinali & Pranit — wedding invitation

A swipe-controlled, bilingual invitation for Jinali Kikani and Pranit Malvania, 26 January 2027.

Open the [live invitation](https://iamutsav27.github.io/jinali-pranit-wedding-invitation/) on a phone. Choose English or ગુજરાતી on the opening screen, then tap to enter with music. Swipe vertically to move through the palace story. The visuals, petals, and doors follow the scroll position, so guests can move forward or back at their own pace.

The Ganpati illustration and JP monogram were created for this invitation. The existing soundtrack plays after the guest taps to enter, as required by mobile browser audio rules.

To open it offline, keep `index.html` and all assets in one folder, then open `index.html`. Google Fonts may use local fallbacks without an internet connection.
